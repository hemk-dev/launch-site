# Notice

launch-site is built from three skills. The originals are unchanged; this folder holds adapted copies and new work.

| Source | Taken (adapted) |
|---|---|
| **website-builder** (no licence file in the copy provided) | The workflow spine; `stacks.md`, `seo.md`, `catalogue.md`, `content-model.md`, `verticals.md`; the budget and quality gate behind `performance.md` and `quality-checklist.md`; `audit_site.py`, `contrast.py`, `screenshots.mjs`; the starting shape of `tokens.css` and `site.config.example.json` |
| **taste-skill** by Leonxlnx (MIT) | Copy rules and tells (`copy.md`, `copy-lint.mjs`); the palette and type library (`library/`), read by the new `brand.mjs`; micro-interaction values and layout rules (`motion.md`, `design-system.md`); the scroll-moment ideas behind `photo-chapters`, `word-highlight`, `picture-develops` and `pinned-steps`; `perf.mjs` |
| **scroll-craft** by Nate Herk (MIT) | The scroll engine (`assets/scroll/scrollcraft.js`, unchanged) and its device styles (`scrollcraft.css`, adapted to the site tokens); the device contract and verification in `scroll-engine.md`; `hero-depth.md`; `serve.mjs`, `scroll-shoot.mjs`, `encode-scrub.sh` |

New in launch-site: `SKILL.md`, `brief-and-planning.md`, `media.md`, the motion tiers, `base.css`, `motion.css`, `site.js`, `starter.html`, `brand.mjs`, and the six recipes in `scroll-engine.md`.
